⭐ Premium

What Premium gives you

  • ⭐ Premium badge next to your name on posts, your profile, and friend lists.
  • Ad-free feed — the ad slots in the feed are hidden for Premium users.
  • Custom profile accent — pick a colour (orange, gold, cyan, pink, purple, or green) that shows as a coloured border and badge on your profile and posts. Premium only.
  • Longer bio — up to 1,000 characters, instead of 500 for free accounts.
  • Explore boost — your posts get a +2 boost to their Explore ranking score, so they rank slightly higher. This boost is shown on your posts and is not hidden.

No price is shown here unless the owner supplies one. The price, if any, is set by the owner in Stripe.
